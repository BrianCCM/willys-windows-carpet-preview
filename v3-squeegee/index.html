Questions, answered

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

It is $50 a room for a professional steam clean. If you want the whole house done, get in touch and we will put a free quote together for you.

How much does window cleaning cost?

Window cleaning is $2 a pane, and that includes getting your screens cleaned as well.

Why does soft water matter?

Hard water leaves mineral deposits behind and can damage what it dries on. We run a soft water filtration system on every job, which is how we get a deeper clean on carpet and a spot free finish on glass.

Do you clean upholstery as well?

Yes. Recliners, sectionals and love seats all get a deep clean on every inch. Upholstery is priced per piece, so reach out for a free quote.

What areas do you serve?

Cedar City and the surrounding Iron County communities. If you are not sure whether you are in range, give us a call and we will tell you straight.
